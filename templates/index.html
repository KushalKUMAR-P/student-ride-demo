How RideLess Works

1️⃣ Post Your Ride

Enter origin, destination, and your budget.

2️⃣ Drivers Accept

Drivers browse and accept rides that fit.

3️⃣ Travel & Pay

Meet up and pay via Zelle or cash.

Why Not Uber?

RideLess flips the model. Instead of algorithmic pricing, students decide the price. No surge. No hidden fees. Pure peer-to-peer rides.
